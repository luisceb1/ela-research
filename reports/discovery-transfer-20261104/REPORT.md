## Prospective question
Do fixed cryptic-splicing predictions enrich for independently observed TDP-43-dependent junction events in genes absent from model fitting and selection, beyond matched sequence/coverage controls?

This is a proposed extension, not a claim that the authors failed to test generalization or that the extension is original. Full methods and broader novelty review are mandatory before execution.

## Frozen decision rules before seeing outcomes
1. Obtain versioned public model weights OR an immutable prediction table, complete fitting/validation/test gene and event lists, assembly/coordinate convention and compatible reuse terms. Hash originals.
2. Obtain independent CULTURE junction evidence with condition, biological-unit and batch mapping. Require at least three independent units per condition and the existing coverage20 criterion. Public tissue catalogues may contextualize identity but do not substitute for the culture contrast or authorize personal data processing.
3. Audit dataset reuse and gene/event overlaps, including validation and hyperparameter selection. Exclude all development genes from the external primary test. Unknown provenance means unavailable, not independent.
4. Freeze one primary score, one external dataset and one detection/coverage denominator before outcomes. Use author-fixed thresholds only when independently specified; otherwise select on development data only.
5. Controls: annotated junctions (pipeline positive controls), known held-out cryptic events (positive controls only), and unannotated sites matched on gene expression, local coverage, splice-site class, intron length and sequence composition. Absence of reads below coverage is missing, not a negative.
6. Primary endpoint: enrichment of observed TDP-43-associated junction events among predicted sites versus matched sites, with gene-blocked uncertainty. Condition effects must respect biological units/batches. Do not treat millions of sites or cells as independent biological replicates.
7. Report all eligible sites and exclusions, threshold sensitivity and leave-one-batch-out stability where design supports it. Predefine multiplicity handling before gene-level candidate testing.
8. Failure: unavailable splits/provenance, inadequate coverage/units, no advantage over matched controls, or direction dependent on one batch. Stop instead of relaxing criteria or replacing the endpoint after inspection.

## Candidate-to-discovery ladder
A model hit is a prediction. Independent junction support is an exploratory candidate. A candidate should survive assembly/strand checks, annotation and published-event exclusion, mapping ambiguity checks and independent biological replication. Protein/function/rescue require separate experiments; no score demonstrates these.
