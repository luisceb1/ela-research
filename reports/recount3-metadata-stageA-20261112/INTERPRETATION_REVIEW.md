## Separate questions and evidential claims

| Question | What one genetic background can support | Additional requirements and limits |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Measurement feasibility/reproduction in a separate experiment:** can the sealed UNC13A cryptic junction be measured with usable support in another public neuronal depletion dataset? | A single background is not automatically disqualifying for this narrow question. Actual junction support can answer a descriptive measurement question conditional on the model. | Verify new experimental observations rather than renamed/reused data, assay/convention/QC, matched conditions, depletion efficacy and a library→culture→differentiation/batch map. A conditional KD/control inference additionally needs genuine experimental replication and its own prospective design; multiple libraries alone do not establish it. No donor-generalization claim. |
| **B. Reproducibility across genetic backgrounds:** is the measurement/response consistent across distinct backgrounds? | A one-background experiment cannot establish cross-background reproducibility by itself. | PR #64 fixed at least three distinct matched backgrounds as its source minimum. Three is a prespecified minimum, not a universal sufficiency theorem, population-generalization result or clinical validation. This screen did not find a qualifying source; it did not prove that one cannot exist or be produced later. |

Independent cultures, independent experimental observations, independent datasets/laboratories and independent donor backgrounds are different axes. Sharing a background does not necessarily mean sharing observations; a new accession, laboratory or paper does not prove new observations. Reprocessing the same measurements is computational reproduction, not biological replication. These distinctions must be fixed before any next analysis.
