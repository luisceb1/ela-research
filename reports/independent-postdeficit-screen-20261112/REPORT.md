## Interpretation and limits

The useful distinction is now explicit: **post-deficit functional recovery is already experimental prior art in TDP-43 transgene-switch models**. It must not be claimed as an invention of this project. Switching off a pathological transgene changes both toxic burden and endogenous TDP-43 regulation; it does not isolate a cryptic-splicing target or establish recovery under persistent TDP-43 dysfunction. The prior six-family UNC13A timing matrix is unchanged: this screen adds different models and cannot be silently appended to its denominator or used to recode its rows.

C1's related Hur 2022 rNLS8 report was read only to check family dependence: Fig.1 describes same-animal gait follow-up, but shares model and investigators with C1. It is **not** a fourth candidate or certified independent replication. Its article lists a supplemental-figures DOCX; no linked numeric table was verified. Spiller follow-ups, Alfieri 2014 and other search returns were not opened as additional candidate dossiers. A different title, year, accession or species does not establish methodological equivalence. Distinct animal species do exclude identity of the animal observations; within-study litter/cage dependence still needs checking.

Access failures are evidence about these retrieval routes only. In particular, Walker's PDF URL returned HTTP 200 **HTML**, not a successful PDF retrieval; no statement about its unseen contents is justified. Matson's indexed abstract cannot establish all methods. Lack of a discovered workbook does not establish that numerical records do not exist. The JCI supplement is a small documentary download (15,235,286 bytes), not a raw experimental-data collection; its SHA256 is recorded. No primary PDF is redistributed in this PR.

No effect, graph digitization, new model, p-value, power calculation, simulation, access request or contact was performed. Missing deficits were not relabelled prevention. Same-cell microtubule measurements, animal motor performance, synaptic currents and clinical benefit remain distinct. This screen was curated by one agent; it is not an independent expert review.

## Recommendation and stop

**Do not start a new effect analysis or extend this search.** Preserve C1 as the first conditional lead if a concrete public or explicitly authorized source later supplies mouse-level week-six and follow-up wirehang/rotarod records with dose timing, control trajectories, experimental grouping and attrition. Only then write a separate prospective protocol for transgene-suppression recovery, clearly labelled as mechanistically different from STMN2/UNC13A-specific rescue. C3 is a secondary lead requiring the analogous rat records; C2 requires accessible methods proving a relevant intervention before any numerical gate.

No new outreach is requested or authorized. Wait for the already pending Wong/Fratta clarifications and actual AD Workbench access. A concrete new artifact may justify a new source decision; elapsed time, an abstract, or a fourth search candidate does not. #37/#44 and the closed 45-donor STMN2 cycle retain their previous gates. The screen is complete with **zero accepted**, not “no opportunities exist.”
