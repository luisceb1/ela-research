# Bounded independent post-deficit source screen

Recorded 12 November 2026, before external candidate searches or adjudication. Base main verified locally and through GitHub: `d09203c8546f3cca7bfeb54911eb322d3451404a`.

## Question and scope

Is there a previously unaudited, independent functional experiment in the TDP-43/STMN2/UNC13A domain in which intervention begins after a measured functional deficit, with sufficient accessible design and numerical metadata to propose a separate validation protocol? This is a documentary/availability review, not an effect analysis or experiment authorization.

## Prior-source exclusion

Read AGENTS, STATE, BACKLOG, PROTOCOL, the originality audit/follow-up, functional evidence/data/access inventories, Chan timing, and the six-family intervention timing matrix. Keuss (all versions), Baughn, Chan, Beccari, Guo (including its final-version access blocker), Mallika/CTR (2025/2026), Cao, Mehta and Gomberg are prior work, not new candidates. NYGC/45 donors, Klim/Melamed RNA cohorts, and #37 technical source gates cannot supply an independent functional intervention by renaming accessions. No replay of closed effects.

## Finite discovery and stopping rule

Use at most four discovery query groups (at most three queries each), aimed at delayed functional recovery with TDP-43 suppression/restoration and STMN2/UNC13A correction. Select at most three plausible previously unaudited study families, in discovery order; exclude a prior family before selection. Inspect primary article methods/legends and linked supplement/data availability (at most two access routes per blocked artifact). A related follow-up is not automatically independent. Stop after the selected families are adjudicated or all four query groups are spent. Do not replace negative selected candidates, chase a fourth family, pay, contact authors, request access, download large experimental data, or fit effects.

## Acceptance and classification

- **Suitable to propose a protocol:** functional deficit measured before first intervention in the same experimental units and endpoint; longitudinal identity/timing and appropriate untreated/vehicle temporal controls verifiable; independent biological units and nesting identifiable; accessible numerical baseline/follow-up and design metadata sufficient to specify an analysis; no demonstrated reuse of prior project observations. Specify target scope: single target, multi-target restoration, or removal of TDP-43 transgene toxicity. These mechanisms are not interchangeable.
- **Pending:** potentially relevant temporal experiment but a necessary baseline/unit/data/independence detail is unavailable or unresolved. State the exact missing input; pending does not authorize outreach or analysis.
- **Discarded for this question:** documented concurrent/pre-deficit intervention, molecular-only endpoint, no intervention, definite data reuse, or an incompatible functional contrast. Missing documentation alone does not prove the deficit was absent; use pending when it is the decisive uncertainty.

Record intervention/specificity; model/function; baseline same-unit/same-endpoint status; first intervention and follow-up; controls/biological replication; accessible numerical artifacts, metadata and restrictions; independence and precise locators. Do not count cells, wells, tests, paper versions or a renamed cohort as independent biological replication. No graph digitization as a substitute for unidentified longitudinal records.

## Refutable prediction and failure criterion

Working possibility: at least one new family satisfies all documentary gates. Failure: zero selected families meets every gate; report no accepted source in this finite screen and close it. Neither result establishes uniqueness, absence of all public opportunities, single-target causality, equivalence, complete recovery or clinical benefit. Preserve negative and uncertain decisions without tuning the gate.
