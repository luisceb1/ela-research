They are technical measurement examples, not a matched culture pair or independent-donor replication. One genetic background, one nominal library per condition; no effect, ratio, PSI, interval, p-value or inference.

Control first: download its two complete FASTQs, verify advertised bytes/MD5 and gzip signature, preprocess and align all retained pairs. Proceed to download KD only if control canonical support is >0; otherwise close as positive-control failure, not cryptic absence. Finish both fixed libraries or stop at first integrity/process/resource failure. No replacement library, retry, resume, alternate mirror or threshold adjustment. Failed attempts consume budget. A small-prefix success does not answer full-library coverage; this executor instead attempts complete libraries, and a partial run has no coverage verdict.

Prediction: resource feasibility and cryptic support may fail; a technically complete result need not reach the historical support diagnostic. Preserve all outcomes. Low canonical counts are not a cryptic-count ceiling or a causal explanation of failure.

Count one support per (contiguous query-name alignment group, sealed event, library), unioning eligible mates. Require exactly one primary read1/read2 pair in the group, paired/proper, mapped, primary/non-supplementary, not QC-failed, NH=1 and 20<=MAPQ<255 on both. HISAT2 --reorder preserves input order; query-group counting is not global UMI/PCR deduplication or proof of molecular independence. Both cryptic events are reported separately, never summed. The historical 20-support diagnostic is labelled with this fragment/group unit and filters, not asserted numerically interchangeable with recount3 read support; it is not an inference gate for these two examples. Canonical >0 is the measurement control, distinct from cryptic support >=20.

## Budget and enforcement

Four fixed FASTQ files advertise 12,826,593,031 bytes. Proposed actual-response body cap **14,000,000,000 bytes**, including incomplete/failed responses; no automatic retry/redirect. 30-second socket timeout; identity content encoding, exact HTTPS URLs and expected length/MD5. No raw transfer during preparation/tests on agent host.

Proposed execution wall cap **12 hours**; run-root cap **100 GiB**; physical Windows C: reserve **50 GiB**. Require a new root and one-time descriptor claim; no overwrite/resume. Two logical CPUs via inherited affinity. Worker/children per-process address-space cap **8 GiB**; sum of process-group RSS sampled <=**6 GiB**. Supervisor samples RSS/root size, elapsed time and actual C: free space, records limits and kills the worker group on violation, timeout or Ctrl+C. Sampled RSS/disk are not hard kernel aggregate quotas and can overshoot between polls; uninterruptible I/O/process escape is not claimed controllable. Failure/partial inputs and receipts are retained; successful plain FASTQ intermediates removed after counting, original verified compressed inputs retained. Logs remain under root and count against disk cap.
