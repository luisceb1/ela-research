## Interpretation and limits

The useful distinction is now explicit: **post-deficit functional recovery is already experimental prior art in TDP-43 transgene-switch models**. It must not be claimed as an invention of this project. Switching off a pathological transgene changes both toxic burden and endogenous TDP-43 regulation; it does not isolate a cryptic-splicing target or establish recovery under persistent TDP-43 dysfunction. The prior six-family UNC13A timing matrix is unchanged: this screen adds different models and cannot be silently appended to its denominator or used to recode its rows.

C1's related Hur 2022 rNLS8 report was read only to check family dependence: Fig.1 describes same-animal gait follow-up, but shares model and investigators with C1. It is **not** a fourth candidate or certified independent replication. Its article lists a supplemental-figures DOCX; no linked numeric table was verified. Spiller follow-ups, Alfieri 2014 and other search returns were not opened as additional candidate dossiers. A different title, year, accession or species does not establish methodological equivalence. Distinct animal species do exclude identity of the animal observations; within-study litter/cage dependence still needs checking.

Access failures are evidence about these retrieval routes only. In particular, Walker's PDF URL returned HTTP 200 **HTML**, not a successful PDF retrieval; no statement about its unseen contents is justified. Matson's complete API abstract describes depletion and stress recovery; it does not describe a qualifying delayed restorative intervention. This warrants abstract-level exclusion from this screen, not the stronger statement that no such experiment exists anywhere in the full paper. Original landing/full-web failures were real route-specific failures, not evidence that the abstract/API was inaccessible. Lack of a discovered workbook does not establish that numerical records do not exist. The JCI supplement is a small documentary download (15,235,286 bytes), not a raw experimental-data collection; its SHA256 is recorded. No primary PDF is redistributed in this PR.

No effect, graph digitization, new model, p-value, power calculation, simulation, access request or contact was performed. Missing deficits were not relabelled prevention. Same-cell microtubule measurements, animal motor performance, synaptic currents and clinical benefit remain distinct. This screen was curated by one agent; it is not an independent expert review.

## Review corrections and disputed proposals

A source-based review on 12 November identified omitted routes and overbroad framing. Walker's design schematic is Fig.6a, whereas functional outcomes are Fig.8f/g. The Results describe grip/rotarod improvement as early as one week; the Fig.8f legend reports the wirehang p<0.001 comparison from three weeks. Preserve both statements: an onset-of-improvement statement and a significance-band statement need not be mutually exclusive. Do not harmonize them into an exact wirehang recovery time or call the difference a proven data error without animal records.

The prior six-source timing matrix contains **six**, not nine, UNC13A-specific interventions. Its supported conclusion is dated and bounded: treatment after a documented functional deficit was not established in those inspected experiments. Neither that matrix nor this screen supports the universal claim that, as of this date, no STMN2/UNC13A-specific experiment exists in public literature. An abstract-only exclusion does not fill the remaining evidence gaps.

The recount3 probe already exists and is useful. However, its Klim result is insufficient **observed cryptic-junction coverage**, not proof that total sequencing depth is the sole cause. The later fraction-discrepancy audit retains partial TDP-43 knockdown, assay, mapping/read-length and biological alternatives. Total reads/read length can prioritize candidates; they cannot guarantee locus coverage or experimental eligibility. A new finite metadata-first screen could be proposed separately, but is not launched or authorized by this correction; #37 remains closed. RNA-junction coverage would address a different question from functional post-deficit recovery. [Detailed correction/proposal assessment](REVIEW_CORRECTIONS.md).
