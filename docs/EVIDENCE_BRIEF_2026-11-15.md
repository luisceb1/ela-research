## The central unresolved question

Why does the selected KD library show STMN2 cryptic support while UNC13A support remains below the descriptive diagnostic? These different loci were measured with their own sealed events; they are not interchangeable assay replicates. The joint observation does not identify locus coverage, depletion strength, genotype, RNA stability/processing or alignment/filter sensitivity as the unique explanation. No cross-gene pooling, new threshold, ratio or sensitivity sweep is justified by the positive control.

Original metadata have been audited for coherence. The agent did not independently replay raw FASTQs or observe host execution. One library per condition in a single genetic background cannot establish an inferential intervention effect, even with millions of eligible read groups.

## Minimum input and prospective decisions

These are **admissibility decisions**, not new effect-analysis protocols. Every admitted input still requires a separate question, estimator/unit, controls, frozen exclusions and stop rule before inspecting target outcomes.

The20paired-query-group threshold is a descriptive support diagnostic for compatible count units, not biological replication or a significance threshold. No input can pass it by changing to PSI, summing different events or treating technical runs as donors.

## Receiving new data without post-hoc adaptation

1. Preserve the received artifact, version/date, source, access restrictions and digest; distinguish a reply from a data delivery.
2. Confirm sample/condition identifiers, experimental units, technical repeats, exclusions, baseline/dose timing where relevant, and overlap with all earlier cohorts.
3. Inspect assay/schema, numerical units, reference/coordinate conventions, missing-data semantics and positive controls. Resolve or explicitly retain uncertainty before outcome extraction.
4. Record a prospective accept/pending/reject decision. For an accepted task, commit its protocol, resource envelope and stop rule before new target analyses. Preserve negative results and amendments.
5. Update current findings and affected summaries together; keep historical entries dated/superseded rather than erase corrections.
