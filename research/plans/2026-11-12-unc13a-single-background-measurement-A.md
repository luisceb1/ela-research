## Units, controls and claim levels fixed before inspection

- Observation unit for this feasibility task: a declared library/run and its exact target-junction read count. One background is permitted for A; it does not establish cross-background reproducibility. Paired FASTQ files, soma/neurite fractions, clones, repeated sequencing and replicate suffixes are not independent cultures.
- Build a technical GSM→SRX→SRR→SRP/project map using primary metadata. Preserve donor/background, culture/differentiation/batch identifiers and unknowns separately. Verify RNA-seq protocol, nominal KD/control, recorded interval and primary evidence for depletion efficacy where available. Do not calculate efficacy from new gene-count files or infer it from shRNA induction alone. A protocol time is not a measured efficacy.
- Check reuse against the existing project inventories and author data statements. A new accession/background/laboratory does not alone establish new observations; record unresolved provenance instead of declaring independence. No pooling of different preparations, compartments or studies.
- **Technical measurement** can be reported descriptively with explicit nominal treatment and unknown biological nesting, provided the library/project identity, assay and count convention are verified. It is not an independent biological effect validation. **Independent-experiment measurement reproduction** additionally requires attributable separate observations and verified perturbation provenance. If those remain unclear, retain the count as technical inspection only; do not promote it.
- **Conditional KD/control inference is not authorized** even if metadata look promising. It would require a separate prospectively specified experimental-unit model and verified independent cultures/differentiations. No p-value, effect estimate, PSI, cross-study recovery ratio, donor-generalization or clinical/functional rescue claim in this task.
